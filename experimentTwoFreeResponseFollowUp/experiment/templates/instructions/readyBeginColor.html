Hello! Remember these instructions (because you're about to be tested on them!)

You have been assigned the role of the speaker . In this task, you will get another participant ("the reader") to click on a target object.

You will know which object is the target because it will have a grey square around it.

Later on, the reader will be shown the same display you were, and they will use what you type to click the target object.

But: the reader won't see the grey box around the target. For them, the objects will be shuffled (so you can't use spatial information like "the one of the left"). They'll have only what you type about the object itself.

There are 48 rounds, which will go by very quickly. When you're ready to be tested on these instructions, click begin.
