READ ME CAREFULLY! MOST IMPORTANT INSTRUCTIONS! YOU WILL BE TESTED ON THEM!

This is your co-worker, Ralph.

Ralph is an expert at identifying the color and material of bats. He can use the color OR material with equal proficiency.

There are three ways Ralph might want you to describe the bat

USING MATERIAL

          Example: "the metal bat"

USING COLOR

          Example: "the blue bat"

USING BOTH COLOR AND MATERIAL

          Example: "the blue metal bat"

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every bat will have a color and a material. But that does not mean you have to mention both the color and the material every single time.

Your job: decide which expression will best help Ralph find the bat in a given batch. Type it into the description for Ralph.

You will get feedback from Ralph. If he finds the bat but it takes him a long time, he'll shrug: ¯\_(ツ)_/¯. If he finds it and is satisfied, he'll give you a thumbs up. If he can't find it, he will give you a thumbs down.
